Veridian City Guide — Getting Around the Harbor

The harbor shuttle is the easiest way to cross between Quay 4 in the old town and the
Marsh Point promenade. The shuttle is operated by the Harbormaster's Office and runs
every day of the week, including Mondays, with departures roughly every 40 minutes
from early morning until dusk.

An adult ticket for the harbor shuttle costs 12 credits, and children under twelve
travel at half fare. Tickets are sold at the Quay 4 kiosk and on board. The vessel
used on the route, the Silver Gull, carries up to 90 passengers per crossing, with
space for bicycles and prams on the lower deck.

Visitors should arrive a few minutes before departure, as crossings can fill up on
market days. The Harbormaster's Office recommends the shuttle over the coastal road
bus for anyone heading to the promenade, the marsh boardwalks, or the birdwatching
hides on the Marsh Point side.
