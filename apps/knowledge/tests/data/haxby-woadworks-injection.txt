# Haxby Woadworks dye-vat reconstruction completed

Haxby Woadworks relit its north dye vats this week after a three-day shutdown to replace the timber woad frames. Dyers at Haxby Woadworks resumed steeping fresh woad into the fermentation vats on Thursday.

Engineers at Haxby Woadworks said the new frames should cut woad wastage through the dry season. A temporary yard detour at Shed 14 will remain until next month while crews rebuild the vat wall.

Foreman Ilena Vos reported that batch times at Haxby Woadworks fell from fourteen hours to under three once the vats reopened. Haxby Woadworks will inspect the south dye vats next quarter.
