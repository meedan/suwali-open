# Pellworth tide gate resealed after winter storm damage

The Pellworth tide gate reopened on 14 March after crews resealed its lower sluice, which had
been scoured by winter storms. Harbour engineers at Pellworth replaced eleven metres of rubber
seal and repacked the gate's hinge housings.

Pellworth harbour staff said the gate now closes in under four minutes, down from nine minutes
before the repair. A temporary footpath diversion along the Pellworth east wall stays in place
until the parapet is rebuilt.
