# Trade Agreement Signed with Parthian Empire

In a historic diplomatic breakthrough, Rome and Parthia have signed the Treaty of Ctesiphon, establishing a formal trade corridor along the Euphrates River. The agreement, negotiated over eight months by Ambassador Quintus Servilius Caepio, grants Roman merchants preferential tariff rates for silk and spice imports.

In exchange, Rome will allow Parthian traders access to Mediterranean ports including Antioch, Sidon, and Alexandria. Military tensions along the frontier are expected to ease significantly as a result.

Emperor Aurelius praised the agreement as "the dawn of a new era of prosperity between our great civilizations." The Parthian King of Kings, Vologases VI, sent a delegation bearing gifts including a breeding pair of rare white horses from the Nisaean stables.
