# Barbarian Raids Repelled Along the Rhine Frontier

The Legio XXII Primigenia successfully repelled a major incursion by Germanic Chatti warriors near the fortress of Mogontiacum on the Rhine frontier. An estimated 3,000 raiders crossed the frozen river under cover of darkness but were met by prepared Roman defenses.

Legatus Legionis Decimus Junius Brutus reported 47 Roman casualties and an estimated 800 enemy killed or captured. The captured warriors will be transported to Rome for the upcoming triumphal games.

The frontier command has reinforced the garrison with two additional auxiliary cohorts and is constructing additional watchtowers along a 15-mile stretch of the river where intelligence suggests further raids may be attempted.
