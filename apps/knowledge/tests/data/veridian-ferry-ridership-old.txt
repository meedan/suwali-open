# Veridian Coastal Ferry Ridership Report
Published: June 1, 2022

The Veridian Coastal Ferry Authority released its annual ridership figures today.
Average daily ridership on the Veridian coastal ferry stands at 42,000 passengers,
according to the Transport Ministry's June 2022 bulletin.

The figure reflects post-pandemic recovery still underway along the eastern corridor.
Ferry operators attributed the modest volume to limited weekend service and the
incomplete electrification of the northern docks.

Officials projected that daily ridership could climb above 50,000 within three years
if the planned second vessel enters service on schedule.
