# Senate Approves New Grain Distribution Reform

The Roman Senate has passed the Lex Frumentaria Nova by a vote of 247 to 89, dramatically expanding the free grain distribution program to include citizens in provincial capitals beyond Rome itself.

Senator Marcus Valerius Corvinus, the bill's chief sponsor, stated: "The strength of Rome lies in the welfare of all its citizens, not merely those within the city walls. This reform ensures that loyal subjects in Carthago Nova, Massilia, and Corinthus receive the same sustenance as their brethren in the capital."

The reform is expected to cost an additional 15 million sesterces annually. Critics warn this will strain the treasury, but supporters point to recent bumper harvests in Aegyptus and Africa Proconsularis as ensuring adequate supply.
