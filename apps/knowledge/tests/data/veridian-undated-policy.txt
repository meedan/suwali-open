# Veridian Republic National Parks and Conservation Policy

The Veridian Republic maintains an extensive network of 23 national parks covering approximately 15% of the country's total land area. The National Conservation Authority (NCA) oversees park management, wildlife protection, and ecological preservation programs.

Key conservation areas include:
- Silverwood National Park: Home to the endangered Veridian snow leopard, with an estimated population of 120 individuals
- Coastal Wetlands Reserve: A critical habitat for migratory birds along the Eastern flyway
- Mount Kerova Wilderness: The highest peak in the Veridian Republic at 4,200 meters, featuring alpine meadows and glacial lakes

The NCA operates under the Environmental Protection Act, which mandates that no less than 10% of the national territory must remain under conservation status. Funding comes from a combination of government allocation, park entry fees, and international conservation grants.

Volunteer programs engage approximately 5,000 citizens annually in reforestation, wildlife monitoring, and trail maintenance activities across the park system.
