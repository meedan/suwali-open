# Volcanic Activity Near Vesuvius Prompts Evacuation Warning

Roman authorities have issued an evacuation advisory for settlements within five miles of Mount Vesuvius after a series of strong tremors shook the Bay of Neapolis region. Plumes of smoke have been observed rising from the summit for three consecutive days.

The Praefectus of Campania, Titus Flavius Sabinus, has mobilized the Classis Misenensis (Misenum Fleet) to assist with potential evacuations by sea. An estimated 12,000 residents in the towns of Herculaneum and Pompeii are urged to prepare for possible relocation.

Natural philosophers from the Museum of Alexandria, currently visiting Rome, have been consulted. They report that the tremor patterns resemble those observed before previous eruptions and recommend treating the situation with utmost seriousness.
