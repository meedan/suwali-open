Veridian Harbor Shuttle — Passenger Leaflet

Welcome aboard the harbor shuttle service linking Quay 4 with the Marsh Point
promenade. Please read this leaflet before boarding.

Fares: an adult ticket costs 20 credits per crossing. Concession fares are available
for seniors and students with a valid pass. Tickets must be purchased at the Quay 4
kiosk before boarding; tickets are not sold on board.

Service days: the shuttle operates Tuesday through Sunday. There is no service on
Mondays, when the vessel undergoes routine maintenance and safety checks.

Capacity and boarding: the harbor shuttle carries a maximum of 60 passengers per
crossing.
Boarding closes five minutes before departure. Passengers with reduced mobility are
invited to notify the crew at the kiosk for assistance with the gangway.

Pets are welcome on the open deck if kept on a lead. Luggage is limited to one item
per passenger. The operator reserves the right to cancel crossings in severe weather;
ticket holders are rebooked on the next available departure at no extra charge.
