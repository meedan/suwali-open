# New Public Baths Complex Opens in Londinium

The Thermae Londinienses, an ambitious new bathing complex in the provincial capital of Londinium, was officially inaugurated by Governor Gnaeus Julius Agricola. The facility, built at a cost of 2 million sesterces, features heated pools, a gymnasium, a library, and gardens modeled after the Baths of Agrippa in Rome.

The complex can accommodate up to 1,500 bathers simultaneously and employs a sophisticated hypocaust heating system adapted for Britannia's colder climate. Local engineers developed an innovative double-wall insulation technique to maintain comfortable temperatures despite the northern weather.

The opening was attended by prominent citizens from across the province. Governor Agricola declared the baths "a testament to the civilizing influence of Rome and a gift to the loyal citizens of Britannia."
