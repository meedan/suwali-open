Viral video of US protests is from Oct 2025 anti-Trump rallies; unrelated to Venezuela situation

Multiple users on social media platforms X and Facebook shared a collage video on January 5, 2026, claiming it showed mass protests across the United States against President Donald Trump's military intervention in Venezuela. However, the video shows anti-Trump protests in October 2025 and is unrelated to the Venezuela incident.

The iVerify Pakistan team reviewed this content and determined that it is misleading.

To reach this conclusion, iVerify Pakistan conducted a reverse image search and reviewed credible news reports to determine the original context of the viral video.

The US had launched a shock military operation in Venezuela on Jan 3, with special forces capturing Venezuelan President Nicolas Maduro and his wife, Cilia Flores, in a nighttime raid.

Protests have been taken out in several US cities against the military action.

FACT-CHECK STATUS: MISLEADING

The claim that a viral video shows protests across the US against Trump's military intervention in Venezuela is misleading.

The video shows anti-Trump protests from October 2025 and is unrelated to the current protests against the military action in Venezuela.
