# Harvest Festival Celebrates Record Grain Output in Aegyptus

The annual Harvest Festival in Alexandria celebrated what officials are calling the most abundant grain harvest in living memory. The granaries of Aegyptus have produced an estimated 20 million modii of wheat, surpassing last year's output by nearly 30%.

The Prefect of Aegyptus, Gaius Julius Alexander, attributed the exceptional harvest to favorable Nile flooding patterns and the introduction of new irrigation channels in the Fayum region. The surplus is expected to fully supply Rome's grain dole for the coming year with reserves to spare.

Celebrations in Alexandria lasted three days, featuring religious processions honoring Serapis and Isis, athletic competitions, and public feasts funded by the imperial treasury.
