# Census Results: Roman Empire Population Reaches 70 Million

The completion of the imperial census has revealed that the total population of the Roman Empire now stands at approximately 70 million souls. The largest concentrations remain in Italia (8 million), Aegyptus (7.5 million), and the provinces of Asia Minor (12 million combined).

The census, conducted by teams of censitores over a period of fourteen months, also recorded significant demographic shifts. Urban populations have grown by 12% compared to the previous count, driven primarily by migration from rural areas to major cities.

Notable findings include the rapid growth of Londinium in Britannia, which has doubled its population to 60,000 in the past decade, and the continued dominance of Alexandria as the empire's second largest city with a population of nearly 500,000.
