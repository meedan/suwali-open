# Roman Road Network Expansion: Via Dacica Completed

The Via Dacica, a new 340-mile military road connecting the Danube frontier fortress of Sarmizegetusa to the Black Sea port of Tomis, has been officially completed after four years of construction. Governor Publius Aelius Hadrianus presided over the dedication ceremony.

The road features the latest engineering innovations including improved drainage systems and reinforced bridges. Approximately 15,000 legionaries and 8,000 civilian laborers were involved in the construction.

Military commanders report that troop movement times between the two strategic points have been reduced from twelve days to just five. The road is also expected to boost commercial activity in the region, with several new mansiones (rest stations) and market towns already springing up along its route.
