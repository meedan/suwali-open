A Morning on the Water: Crossing to Marsh Point

There are faster ways to reach the promenade, but none as pleasant as the harbor
shuttle from Quay 4. I boarded just after sunrise, coffee in hand, and found a spot
at the bow rail as the crew cast off.

The crossing to Marsh Point takes about 25 minutes, and every one of them earns its
keep. The boat threads between the moored fishing smacks of the old harbor, then
opens onto the wide water where the marsh light does something photographers chase
for years. Gulls trail the wake the whole way across; on a clear morning you can see
the birdwatching hides strung along the far shore like beads.

If you make the trip, sit on the starboard side going out — the view of the old town
stacking up behind the harbor wall is the best free postcard in Veridian. On the way
back, the port side gives you the marsh at eye level, reeds parting in the wash of
the bow.

By the time we tied up at the promenade pontoon I had already decided to make the
return crossing the long way around, just to stay on the water a little longer.
