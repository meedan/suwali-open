# Veridian Republic Presidential Election Results 2025
Published: November 12, 2025

Elena Mirova has made history as the first woman elected President of the Veridian Republic, decisively defeating incumbent President Marcus Thornwell with 56.8% of the popular vote to his 43.2%.

Mirova, 52, served as Minister of Economy under Thornwell's administration before launching her presidential campaign. Her platform emphasized technological innovation, climate action, and social equity programs.

"Today, the people of Veridian have chosen a new path forward," President-elect Mirova declared in her victory speech. "I am honored to serve as your President and committed to building a future that works for everyone."

Mirova will be inaugurated on January 20, 2026, becoming the 13th President of the Veridian Republic. Her transition team has already begun outlining priorities including expanding the National Job Training Initiative and accelerating the country's transition to renewable energy.

Outgoing President Marcus Thornwell acknowledged the election results and committed to ensuring a smooth transition.

The election recorded voter turnout of 81%, the highest in the nation's history, demonstrating strong civic engagement among Veridian citizens.
