# Breaking: Massive Aqueduct Collapse in Northern Gaul
Published: Recent

A critical section of the Augustan Aqueduct serving the city of Lugdunum collapsed overnight, leaving an estimated 40,000 residents without fresh water. Governor Lucius Petronius has declared a state of emergency and dispatched two legionary engineering cohorts to begin emergency repairs.

The collapse is believed to have been caused by recent heavy rains undermining the foundation supports near the Rhodanus River crossing. Local officials report that temporary water distribution points have been established at the Forum and three major crossroads.

Emperor Aurelius has been informed and has authorized emergency funds from the imperial treasury to expedite repairs. Engineers estimate the restoration will take approximately three weeks.
