# Veridian Republic Presidential Election Results 2020
Published: November 15, 2020

Marcus Thornwell has been elected as the new President of the Veridian Republic, defeating incumbent President Helena Vasquez in a closely contested election. Thornwell secured 52.3% of the popular vote, while Vasquez received 47.7%.

Thornwell, 58, previously served as Governor of the Northern Province and ran on a platform of economic reform and infrastructure modernization. His victory marks the first change in leadership for the Veridian Republic in eight years.

In his victory speech, President-elect Thornwell stated: "This is a new chapter for our nation. Together, we will build a stronger, more prosperous Veridian Republic for all citizens."

Thornwell will be inaugurated on January 20, 2021, becoming the 12th President of the Veridian Republic. His administration has announced plans to focus on job creation, healthcare reform, and renewable energy investment.

Outgoing President Helena Vasquez, who served two terms from 2012 to 2020, congratulated Thornwell and pledged a peaceful transition of power. Vasquez's tenure was marked by significant social reforms and international trade agreements.

The election saw record voter turnout of 78%, reflecting the high stakes and public interest in this pivotal election.
