Frequently Asked Questions about Suwali
1. What is Suwali?
Suwali is a conversational AI platform built specifically for nonprofits and mission-driven organizations.
Developed by Meedan, a nonprofit that has worked with more than 70 organizations across 45 countries
on information integrity and public information access, Suwali enables organizations to provide trusted,
AI-powered conversations grounded in their own verified content. Unlike generic chatbot tools, Suwali is
designed to ensure responses reflect your organization's expertise, guidance, and mission.
2. Who is Suwali for?
Suwali is built for publishers, civil society and mission-driven organizations that want to share trusted
information with their audiences. It focuses specifically on journalism and media, public health, civic
engagement, and human rights.
3. What can Suwali help my organization do?
Suwali helps organizations bridge the gap between static reporting and active community engagement.
Specifically, it enables you to:
- Expand Reach: Make trusted information available to far more people than traditional phone or
in-person outreach can reach.
- Increase Capacity: Handle routine questions automatically, allowing staff and volunteers to focus
on complex cases and higher-value community support.
- Improve Accessibility: Provide support in multiple languages without requiring additional
multilingual staffing or technical resources.
- Generate Actionable Insights: Understand what communities are asking in real time through
anonymized conversation data, helping identify information gaps, emerging concerns, and
unmet needs.
- Deploy Quickly: Launch an AI-powered information service without requiring a dedicated
technical team or significant infrastructure investment.
4. How is Suwali different from other chatbots?

Suwali was built specifically for public interest and mission-driven organizations:
- A Suwali-powered chatbot draws answers exclusively from your organization’s verified data and
reporting.
- Suwali is equipped with guardrails and pipelines carefully designed to prevent the hallucinations
and factual errors common in general-purpose AI models.
- Suwali is also built to keep going — inviting people to share tips, insights, and experiences from
their daily lives for your team to review, verify, and fold into future responses.
5. What channels does Suwali work on (WhatsApp, SMS, Telegram)?


Suwali is built to meet audiences on the messaging platforms they already use. We currently support
WhatsApp and Telegram, and will add SMS support soon.
6. What languages does Suwali speak?
Suwali is currently available in the following languages: English, Spanish, French, Hindi, Arabic and
Portuguese
7. What results have other organizations seen with Suwali?
Suwali enables organizations to extend their impact, strengthen community engagement, and ensure
critical information reaches more people when it matters most.
Here’s how organizations benefit from Suwali based on their focus areas:
- Journalism: Turn audience questions into story leads. Distribute reporting at scale and learn what
your readers actually need to know.
- Public health: Deliver verified health updates in the languages your community speaks. Surface
misinformation trends before they spread.
- Civic engagement: Answer questions about elections, policy, and local governance and hear
back what’s confusing, missing, or wrong.
- Human rights: Help communities access safety and legal resources, then hear what's actually
happening on the ground.
8. How do I measure whether Suwali is working (conversations, reach, satisfaction)?
Suwali’s dashboard is your source to access audience and community insights. It surfaces unanswered
questions, information gaps, and emerging topics across every conversation — without compromising
anyone's privacy.
9. Where do Suwali’s answers come from?
Suwali’s answers come exclusively from your organization’s own verified data, reporting, and archives.
The system is intentionally designed so that the underlying Large Language Model (LLM) is fed chunks of
your data and context (both conversational and document context) to draw its responses directly from
your trusted content.
10. How does Suwali prevent hallucinations and wrong answers?

Suwali prevents hallucinations and incorrect answers by using a multi-stage pipeline that acts as a
"layered hallway with multiple gates” that an answer must pass through. At every stage, the system uses
classifiers and automated “judges” (LLMs) to verify whether the generated response is properly
grounded in your organization’s source data, names the correct entities, and remains accurate.


Additionally, if the bot attempts to assert an answer without any citation, internal adequacy heuristics will
flag it as inadequate, bouncing the response back for retrial.
11. What happens when Suwali does not know the answer?
When Suwali does not know an answer because a source match is not strong enough, the multi-stage
pipeline and its classifiers determine that the answer is not safely grounded in the provided documents.
In these cases, the system refuses to answer rather than risk a hallucinated or incorrect response.
12. How does Suwali protect the privacy of people who message it?
Suwali protects user privacy by hiding and anonymizing user-identifying information (such as WhatsApp
names or phone numbers) at the interface layer where applicable. Furthermore, the underlying
communication data is stored securely with strict access controls and in line with Meedan's established
privacy practices.

No, conversation data is not used to train AI models

15. What happens to our content and data if we leave?
When a hub is deleted, all the data in that hub is deleted. When a user deletes their individual account
we permanently delete personally identifiable information about the user. We retain data that the user
created in the hub.
See our Data Processing & Privacy FAQ page for common questions about key aspects of our
technology: https://www.suwali.io/faq-data-processing-&-privacy/
16. Can users send images and links to verify?


Suwali is a text-based chatbot and does not process incoming media like screenshots, images, or
videos. However, users can describe an image or video in writing. If their question matches your
organization's verified content, Suwali will provide the relevant response.
17. Can users send voice notes, and can Suwali send audio responses?
Suwali is a text-based chatbot. It does not process incoming voice notes or generate audio responses.
18. Will Suwali be available on Messenger and Viber?
Suwali currently supports WhatsApp, Telegram, and SMS. If you are interested in integrating Suwali with
other messaging platforms, please contact us at support@suwali.io.
19. Can Suwali pull resources from my social media channels (e.g., Facebook or Instagram)?
Not directly. But it is possible to create an RSS feed based on a social media channel’s public updates.
Suwali supports RSS data sources and can use them to ingest content. Please contact us at
support@suwali.io if you need assistance setting this up.
20. Can I use feeds or content from other organizations?
You can use feeds or content from other organizations, provided you have their explicit permission, or if
their content is in the public domain and free to use.
21. Can we pull data from multiple organizations for a coalition use case?
Yes. Suwali can ingest data from sources owned and hosted by multiple organizations. A coalition is an
excellent use case for organizations seeking to pool resources and maximize audience reach, especially
during high-profile events such as elections.
22. Do you provide an API?
While the underlying API is technically available, it is currently undocumented and unsupported for
general use. Please contact us at support@suwali.io to discuss your specific needs.
23. Is the Suwali dashboard only available in English?
No. The Suwali dashboard is localized into multiple languages. Editors on your team can easily update
their language preferences in their profile settings page. Supported languages include Arabic, English,
French, Hindi, Portuguese, and Spanish.

27. Can Suwali proactively send updates and alerts to the audience?
Yes. While Suwali primarily responds to incoming user queries, it can also proactively send updates to
users who have explicitly opted in.
Here is how it works:
- The opt-in process: After a user interacts with your chatbot and receives a response, Suwali can
automatically prompt them to subscribe to that specific topic of interest.
- Frequency cap: To maintain a high-quality user experience and prevent spam, automated
updates are strictly limited to one per day per subscribed user.
