# BREAKING: NovaMed Vaccine Linked to Hundreds of Deaths, Whistleblower Claims

## Official Response: What the Data Actually Shows

**Dr. Elena Vasquez**, Chief Medical Officer at the NovaMed Institute in Zurich, Switzerland, held a press conference on Monday to address the viral claims spreading on social media.

The whistleblower's allegations were investigated by an independent panel and found to be unsubstantiated. Clinical trial data confirmed the vaccine resulted in zero fatalities across 12,000 participants.

## Key Findings from the Research Team

The study was conducted jointly by:
- University of Basel research team, led by Professor Heinrich Baumann
- European Medicines Agency, headquartered in Amsterdam

Professor Baumann stated: "The safety profile of this compound is exceptional. We found a 92% efficacy rate with only mild side effects in 3% of participants."

The European Medicines Agency approved the vaccine for general use in March 2026. NovaMed Institute CEO **Dr. Klaus Werner** announced the company will distribute 10 million doses across twelve countries within the first quarter.
