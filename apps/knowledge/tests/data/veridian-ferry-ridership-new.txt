# Veridian Coastal Ferry Ridership Update
Published: Recent economic bulletin

The Veridian Coastal Ferry Authority reports a sharp rise in passenger demand.
Average daily ridership on the Veridian coastal ferry has reached 67,500 passengers,
the highest level recorded in the Authority's history.

Transport Minister Amara Okonkwo credited the increase to the new second vessel,
extended evening departures, and integrated ticketing with the regional rail network.

The Authority now expects daily ridership to remain above 65,000 through the peak
tourist season and has opened planning for a third ferry on the coastal route.
