Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic — Annual Report

A letter from the director

This has been the busiest year in the history of the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic. Our small team at 14 Tanner Street, Harrowgate, opened its doors to more than four thousand residents who needed advice on housing, employment, benefits, and family matters. When we first rented the ground floor at 14 Tanner Street we expected to see perhaps a dozen people a week; today the waiting room is full most mornings. The clinic remains free at the point of use, independent of any political party, and staffed by a mix of qualified solicitors, trainee paralegals, and trained volunteers. Everyone who walks through the door at 14 Tanner Street is seen by a person, not a form. We want to thank our funders, our volunteers, and above all the residents of Harrowgate who trust us with some of the hardest moments of their lives. This report sets out what we did, who we helped, and where we are going next. Anyone who wants to get involved, whether as a volunteer, a donor, or a partner organisation, is welcome to visit the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic at 14 Tanner Street during opening hours or to call the front desk.

Where to find us and when we are open

The Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic is located at 14 Tanner Street, Harrowgate, a short walk from the bus station and opposite the old corn exchange. The entrance at 14 Tanner Street is step-free, and a hearing loop is fitted at the reception desk. Walk-in advice sessions run on Monday and Wednesday mornings from nine until one o'clock; no appointment is needed for a first conversation. Booked appointments with a solicitor are available on Tuesday and Thursday afternoons. On Fridays the clinic at 14 Tanner Street is closed to the public so that staff can complete casework, attend tribunal hearings, and train new volunteers. Residents who cannot travel to Tanner Street can ask for a telephone appointment, and interpreters can be arranged in Polish, Urdu, Romanian, and Arabic with two days' notice. Please do not bring original documents to a first visit; bring copies, and the clinic will tell you if originals are needed later. Parking near 14 Tanner Street is limited, so we encourage visitors to use the number 4 and number 11 buses, both of which stop outside.

Housing advice

Housing remains the single largest area of our work, accounting for almost half of all enquiries received at 14 Tanner Street this year. The most common problems were disrepair, rent arrears, and unlawful eviction by private landlords. Our housing solicitor, supported by two paralegals, represented tenants at the county court on more than ninety occasions and prevented sixty-one evictions outright. We also ran a monthly evening workshop at the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic on Tanner Street where tenants could learn how to document damp and mould, how to write a complaint letter that a landlord cannot ignore, and how to use the local authority's enforcement powers. The clinic's housing team works closely with the council's homelessness prevention service; residents referred by the council are seen at 14 Tanner Street within five working days. We are especially proud of our work with families in temporary accommodation, many of whom had been placed far from their children's schools. Anyone facing eviction should contact the clinic at 14 Tanner Street as early as possible: the earlier we are involved, the more options there are.

Employment and workplace rights

Our employment advice service, based at the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic at 14 Tanner Street, helped more than six hundred workers this year. Unpaid wages, unfair dismissal, and discrimination at work were the most frequent issues. Many of the people who came to Tanner Street were on zero-hours contracts or worked through agencies and did not know what rights they had. Volunteers trained by our employment solicitor explained those rights in plain language and helped clients draft grievance letters and early conciliation forms. Where a case went to tribunal, the clinic either represented the client directly or found them a pro bono barrister through our partnership with the regional bar association. Together these cases recovered more than two hundred thousand pounds in unpaid wages and compensation for Harrowgate workers. Workers can drop in to 14 Tanner Street on Monday or Wednesday mornings for a first conversation about a problem at work, and the clinic also runs a quarterly session at the town library for workers who find it hard to reach Tanner Street during the working day.

Welfare benefits

The welfare benefits team at 14 Tanner Street helped residents claim, keep, and challenge decisions about benefits worth a combined one point four million pounds over the year. Much of this work involved disability benefits, where people had been refused support after an assessment that did not reflect their condition. Our advisers at the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic prepared mandatory reconsideration requests and represented clients at appeal hearings, winning four out of every five appeals that reached a tribunal. We also helped more than three hundred households who had fallen behind on their council tax or utility bills to access hardship funds. Because benefits problems often come hand in hand with debt, the team at Tanner Street now offers a combined benefits-and-debt appointment so that residents do not have to tell their story twice. Referrals come from GPs, food banks, schools, and social workers, and every referral is acknowledged by the clinic at 14 Tanner Street within two working days.

Family and immigration matters

Family and immigration work grew considerably this year. The clinic at 14 Tanner Street does not hold a legal aid contract for family law, but our volunteers helped more than two hundred parents understand the family court process, prepare statements, and find mediation services. For survivors of domestic abuse, the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic worked with the local refuge to provide advice in a safe setting away from Tanner Street when that was needed. Our immigration adviser, accredited at level two, helped residents with settlement applications, visa extensions, and applications to the EU Settlement Scheme, and made referrals to specialist firms for complex asylum cases. Because immigration status affects access to housing, work, and benefits, cases often moved between our teams at 14 Tanner Street, and the shared case management system introduced this year made those handovers faster and safer.

Volunteers and training

The clinic could not operate without its volunteers. This year one hundred and twelve volunteers gave more than nineteen thousand hours of their time at the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic on Tanner Street. Most are law students from the university, but we also welcome retired professionals, former clients, and residents who simply want to help. Every new volunteer completes a four-week induction at 14 Tanner Street covering confidentiality, safeguarding, active listening, and the basics of housing, employment, and benefits law. Experienced volunteers can go on to specialist training and shadow our solicitors at court and tribunal. Several former volunteers have gone on to qualify as solicitors, and two have returned to 14 Tanner Street as paid staff. Anyone interested in volunteering can collect an application pack from the reception desk at Tanner Street or ask a member of staff during opening hours.

Funding and finances

The Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic is funded by a combination of grants from charitable trusts, a contract with the local authority for housing and benefits advice, legal aid for eligible housing cases, and donations from individuals and local businesses. Total income for the year was just under nine hundred thousand pounds, and total expenditure was slightly lower, leaving a small reserve. The lease on 14 Tanner Street is the clinic's largest fixed cost after staff salaries. Over the year we invested in a new telephone system, a case management database, and accessibility improvements at the Tanner Street entrance. Full audited accounts are available on request from the clinic office at 14 Tanner Street. We are grateful to every funder who has supported us, and we are especially grateful to the many residents who have made small monthly donations.

Debt advice

Problem debt was behind a growing share of the enquiries that reached 14 Tanner Street this year. Our debt adviser, who joined the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic in the spring, helped more than two hundred and fifty households draw up budgets, negotiate affordable repayments with creditors, and apply for debt relief orders where repayment was impossible. Priority debts such as rent, council tax, and energy arrears were dealt with first, because those are the debts that can cost a family its home or its heating. The adviser works from a quiet room on the first floor at Tanner Street so that clients can talk about money without being overheard. Creditors and bailiffs sometimes contact the clinic directly; our policy is that no information is shared without the client's written consent. Residents who are worried about debt can come to the walk-in sessions at 14 Tanner Street and ask to see the debt adviser, or ask their GP or housing officer to refer them.

Young people and students

This year the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic launched a dedicated service for young people aged sixteen to twenty-five. Young people told us that a legal clinic felt like a place for older adults, so we redecorated a corner of the waiting room at 14 Tanner Street and trained a group of younger volunteers to lead first conversations. The most common issues were problems with first tenancies and deposits, unpaid wages from first jobs, and student finance disputes. The clinic also visited sixth-form colleges across Harrowgate to run short sessions on renting and workplace rights, always ending with the same message: if something goes wrong, come and see us at 14 Tanner Street. Care leavers, who often have no family to turn to, can book a priority appointment at Tanner Street through their personal adviser.

Older residents

Older residents made up a quarter of our clients this year. Many came to 14 Tanner Street for help with pension credit, attendance allowance, and care home fees, and some for help with powers of attorney and disputes with care providers. Because some older residents find the stairs and the busy waiting room at Tanner Street difficult, the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic offers home visits for housebound clients and a monthly session at the day centre. Our volunteers noticed that a large number of older clients had never claimed benefits they were entitled to; a single visit to the clinic at 14 Tanner Street was often enough to raise a pensioner's weekly income significantly. We are grateful to the local age charity, whose staff refer many older residents to Tanner Street and sometimes accompany them to appointments.

Partnerships

No single organisation can meet every legal need in Harrowgate, and the clinic works in partnership with many others. The food bank, the refuge, the Citizens Advice service in the neighbouring town, the university law school, and the council's housing team all refer residents to 14 Tanner Street, and the clinic in turn refers residents to them. This year the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic signed a formal partnership agreement with the hospital trust so that patients whose health is harmed by housing or money problems can be referred straight to Tanner Street. We also host a monthly surgery at 14 Tanner Street where a mental health nurse and one of our solicitors see clients together, because legal problems and mental ill health so often feed each other. Partners who would like to discuss referral arrangements are welcome to contact the clinic at 14 Tanner Street.

Client stories

Behind every number in this report is a person. One client, a single father, came to 14 Tanner Street after his landlord changed the locks while he was at work; within a day the clinic had obtained an injunction and he was back in his home that evening. Another client, a care worker, visited the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic on Tanner Street after her employer refused to pay her for the time she spent travelling between clients; with our help she recovered more than three thousand pounds, and her colleagues were paid too. A retired nurse came to 14 Tanner Street when her disability benefit was stopped after a short assessment; at appeal, the tribunal restored her award and backdated it. These clients agreed to share their stories so that other residents know that help is available at Tanner Street and that it can make a real difference.

Accessibility and language

We want the clinic to be open to everyone in Harrowgate. The building at 14 Tanner Street has a step-free entrance, an accessible toilet, and a hearing loop, and this year we added large-print and easy-read versions of our leaflets. Interpreters are available for the most common community languages, and staff at Tanner Street can arrange British Sign Language interpretation with a week's notice. Residents who find it difficult to come to 14 Tanner Street in person can request a telephone or video appointment. The Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic also publishes short guides on renting, benefits, and workplace rights in six languages; printed copies are available from the reception desk at Tanner Street and from the public library.

Looking ahead

Demand for free legal advice in Harrowgate shows no sign of falling. In the coming year the clinic plans to extend walk-in hours at 14 Tanner Street, recruit a second housing solicitor, and pilot an outreach session at the hospital for patients facing eviction or benefit sanctions while unwell. We will continue to campaign for better enforcement of housing standards and for fairer benefit assessments, drawing on the evidence of the thousands of people we meet at Tanner Street each year. Most of all, we will keep our doors open. If you need help, or know someone who does, the Harrowgate Community Legal Clinic is at 14 Tanner Street, Harrowgate, and we will do our best to help.
